How an Old Keyboard Is Transformed into a Pixel Art Wall Decoration
Paragraph 1 - General Statement Electronic waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the rapid development of technology. Old computer keyboards are often discarded when they are damaged or no longer needed. However, these keyboards contain materials that can still be reused. One creative way to manage this waste is by transforming old keyboard keys into a unique pixel art wall decoration, specifically forming a heart shape, which carries high aesthetic value while supporting eco-friendly recycling movements.
Paragraph 2 - The Transformation Process The creation process begins with the preparation of materials and tools, including used computer keyboard keys in various colors, a base board (MDF/acrylic), strong adhesive (hot glue/Fox), a ruler, a pencil, and a cleaning cloth. The transformation process starts when the used keyboard keys are thoroughly cleaned and dried from dust using a cleaning cloth. Next, guide lines or grids are created on the base board using a ruler and a pencil to ensure the key arrangement remains straight.
Paragraph 3 - The Formation of a New Product After preparing the base, the cleaned keyboard keys are arranged and glued onto the board according to the specific pattern of a heart shape. The combination of different colored keys creates an interesting 3D textured visual effect. Once all the keys are neatly arranged following the pattern, they are firmly attached using the adhesive, and any leftover glue residue is cleaned during the finishing stage.
Paragraph 4 - The Result and Its Impact As a result, an unused computer keyboard is successfully transformed into a creative pixel art wall decoration with both functional and aesthetic appeal. Production costs total Rp30,000 for materials, and with a selling price of Rp45,000, it generates a profit of Rp15,000 per product. Furthermore, this transformation reduces electronic waste and encourages the reuse of available materials to support a more sustainable environment.

Muhammad Alva Naja Izzani

General Statement Over time, waste and trash continue to accumulate—a phenomenon that can occur anywhere, including in computer labs. For this reason, we should repurpose the waste found in such environments. Take keyboards, for example: we can reuse old, discarded keyboards by transforming them into something useful—such as the pixel art wall decor we offer. Our product is crafted from repurposed keyboard keys, which are cleaned and arranged to form specific images or designs.

The Transformation Process The process of creating this pixel art wall decoration involves only two steps, as it is quite simple to make:

  1. Removing the keys from a keyboard
  2. Cleaning the keyboard keys

The Formation of a New Product Once the keyboard keys have been removed and cleaned, we can begin creating the wall decoration. This process is quite challenging, as it requires both precision and patience. The steps are as follows:

  1. Create a sketch of the desired image.
  2. Construct a frame and a backing that suit the image.
  3. Attach the keyboard keys according to the sketch.
  4. Apply color to the keys to match the design.
  5. Fit the frame onto the piece and display it on the wall.

The Result and Its Impact The result is a beautiful wall decoration that is pleasing to the eye; furthermore, this activity helps reduce waste and fosters individual creativity.

Hanifatul CHasanah

General Overview Electronic waste and old keyboard parts are often discarded and overlooked, yet they hold hidden potential. Many people simply throw away outdated or broken keyboards, contributing to growing piles of waste that harm our environment. However, with creativity and effort, these unwanted items can be transformed into something beautiful and meaningful.

The Transformation Process The process starts by collecting keys from unused or broken keyboards, cleaning them carefully to remove dust and dirt. Next, arrange and fit the keys together to form a specific shape or design—such as a heart, symbol, or pattern—working piece by piece until the form is complete. Meanwhile, prepare a canvas or board and paint it with a colorful background that complements the arrangement, adding depth and beauty to the overall look.

Creating New Pieces Once the background paint has dried, carefully attach the arranged keyboard keys onto the surface using strong adhesive. Take time to ensure each key is securely fixed and the design looks balanced and neat. The contrast between the colorful painted background and the dark keyboard keys creates a striking visual effect, turning simple electronic scraps into unique pixel art that catches the eye and sparks admiration.

Results and Impact The final result is a stunning wall decoration—a heart-shaped artwork made entirely from recycled keyboard keys—that brings a modern, artistic touch to any room. It proves that waste does not have to be wasted. By reusing materials that would otherwise end up in landfills, we reduce electronic waste, protect the environment, and inspire others to see value in things that others have thrown away.
